Activities & Experiences

Things to do in Davao Region, from mountain trails to local food.

Which traveler are you?

🧗 The adventurer I want mountains and waves. 🎭 The culture lover I want festivals and stories. 🍴 The foodie I want to taste everything. 🌳 The relaxer I want cool air and quiet beaches.
🧗

Adventure

For people who like climbing, swimming and surfing.

  • ⛰ Hike up Mount Apo, the highest mountain in the Philippines. ChallengingNeeds a guide
  • 🧭 Trek the mossy forest of Mount Hamiguitan. ChallengingNeeds a guide
  • 🏄 Try surfing or skimboarding at Dahican Beach in Mati. ModerateBeginner friendly
  • 🚢 Go island hopping and snorkeling around Samal Island. EasyFamily friendly
Good to know

Mountain climbs need advance arrangements, so book early and check the current permit rules.

🎭

Culture

Festivals, traditions and the people of the region.

  • 🎉 Join the Kadayawan Festival in August, a celebration of harvest and culture. Yearly event
  • 🏛 Learn about the Bagobo, Mandaya and other indigenous peoples of the region. Learn something
  • 🖼 Visit a local museum in Davao City to see the history of the region. Indoors
  • 🦅 Meet the Philippine Eagle at the Philippine Eagle Center in Calinan. Family friendly
Good to know

Festival dates change from year to year, so check the schedule before you book your trip.

🍴

Food

Fresh fruit, seafood and local chocolate.

  • 🍇 Taste durian, pomelo and other local fruits in season. Seasonal
  • 🐟 Order grilled tuna, a Davao favorite at many local restaurants. Must try
  • 🍫 Try chocolate made from locally grown cacao. Good gift
  • 🥤 Buy fresh fruit and snacks to take home from a local market. Budget friendly
Good to know

Durian has a very strong smell, so some hotels and buses do not allow it. Ask first.

🌳

Nature & Relaxation

Slow days, cool air and quiet beaches.

  • 🧺 Have a picnic and walk the gardens at Eden Nature Park. Family friendly
  • 🏖 Spend a lazy afternoon on the white sand beaches of Samal. Easy day
  • 🐢 Watch for sea turtle hatchling releases at Dahican Beach. Seasonal
  • 🌅 Watch the sunrise over the Pacific on the Davao Oriental coast. Early start
Good to know

The highlands are cooler than the coast, so bring a light jacket for Eden Nature Park.

A sample 3-day trip

This is how I would mix the activities above. Change it to fit your own time.

  1. Day 1

    Davao City highlands

    Visit the Philippine Eagle Center in the morning, then have a picnic and a walk at Eden Nature Park. End the day with grilled tuna for dinner.

  2. Day 2

    Samal Island

    Take the ferry for a day of island hopping, snorkeling and a lazy afternoon on the beach.

  3. Day 3

    Davao Oriental coast

    Head to Mati for a morning surf or skimboard lesson at Dahican Beach. Ask locally if a turtle release is happening that day.
