Popular Destinations

Six places you should not miss in Davao Region.

  • Mount Apo
  • Samal Island
  • Eagle Center

Mount Apo

Davao City and Davao del Sur

Mount Apo is the highest mountain in the Philippines, with a peak at about 2,954 meters. Hikers climb it for the crater, the sulfur vents and the forest trails.

Travel tip

Climbs are arranged through registered guides, so check the permit rules first.

Samal Island

Island Garden City of Samal, Davao del Norte

Samal Island is a short ferry ride from Davao City. It has white sand beaches, resorts, caves and clear water for swimming and snorkeling.

Travel tip

Go on a weekday if you want a quieter beach.

Philippine Eagle Center

Malagos, Calinan, Davao City

This center takes care of the Philippine Eagle, the national bird of the country. Visitors can see the eagles up close and learn how they are protected.

Travel tip

Visit in the morning when it is cooler and the birds are more active.

Dahican Beach

Mati City, Davao Oriental

Dahican Beach is a long stretch of sand on the Pacific coast. It is popular for surfing and skimboarding, and sea turtle hatchlings are released here.

Travel tip

Turtle releases depend on the season, so ask locally first.

See on Google Maps

Mount Hamiguitan

San Isidro, Davao Oriental

Mount Hamiguitan Range Wildlife Sanctuary is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is known for its mossy forest and for rare plants and animals.

Travel tip

Treks need advance arrangements, so contact the local office first.

See on Google Maps

Eden Nature Park

Toril, Davao City

Eden Nature Park is in the highlands above Davao City, where the air is cool and pine trees line the paths. Families come for the gardens, viewpoints and picnic areas.

Travel tip

Bring a light jacket because it gets chilly up there.
