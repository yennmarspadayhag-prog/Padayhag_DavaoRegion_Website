Travel Basics

📅

When to go

August is the busiest and most colorful month because of the Kadayawan Festival. Book early if you want to be there.

✈

Getting there

Most visitors fly into Davao International Airport. From Davao City you can travel on to the other provinces by road.

🚕

Getting around

Use taxis, ride-hailing apps or jeepneys in the city. Samal Island is a short ferry ride from Davao City.

🏨

Where to stay

Davao City is the easiest base. For beach days, stay on Samal or in Mati.

Good to Know

Do I need a guide for mountain climbs?

Climbs of Mount Apo and Mount Hamiguitan are arranged through registered guides and local offices. Book early and check the current permit rules.

Can I bring durian to my hotel?

Many hotels and buses do not allow durian because of its strong smell. Ask first.

When can I see sea turtle hatchlings at Dahican?

Releases depend on the season, so ask locally before you go.

Do festival dates stay the same every year?

No. Check the schedule before you book your trip.
